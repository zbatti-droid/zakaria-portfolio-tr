Dar Sidi Bibi was designed around a real restaurant workflow: customers need a clear menu and reservation path while staff need simple administration tools.

Next.js, TypeScript, Prisma, PostgreSQL and Supabase support the product. Arabic uses RTL layout and French uses LTR layout, while menu, gallery, reservation and WhatsApp actions stay consistent across mobile and desktop.
