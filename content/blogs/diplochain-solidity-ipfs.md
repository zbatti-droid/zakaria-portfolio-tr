DiploChain is my final-year project at FST Errachidia, graded 16/20. I built a web flow where diplomas can be issued, stored and verified with a clear audit trail.

The application combines React and Vite on the frontend, Node.js and Express APIs, PostgreSQL logs, Solidity smart contracts, IPFS through Pinata and MetaMask wallet actions. QR verification and role-based access keep the workflow practical for students, employers and administrators.
